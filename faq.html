SunTrace - FAQ and contact

SunTrace is an analog watch face for round Garmin watches (Connect IQ) that follows the sun: a small sun travels along a thin arc over a tropical infinity-pool landscape, from sunrise to sunset. After sunset it turns into the moon and the face switches to a night scene. It is free to try; one payment through KiezelPay unlocks it for good.

How does the trial and the payment work?

The free trial starts when you install the watch face. When it ends, the watch shows a code and the address www.kzl.io/code. Open that address on your phone or computer, enter the code and pay once; the watch then stays unlocked for good.

I paid but the watch still shows the code

Keep the watch connected to your phone. The watch checks the purchase about every 5 minutes, so it can take a few minutes to unlock. If it still shows the code after 15 minutes, restart the watch face (switch to another one and back) and wait a little longer.

Which watches are supported?

Round AMOLED Garmin watches listed on the Connect IQ Store page of the app. More models are added over time. Watches with the older MIP screens are not supported because the scenery does not look good on them.

What does the watch face show?

An analog clock with the hour numerals 12, 3, 6 and 9, a second hand, and three round complications: heart rate, Body Battery and steps. Body Battery and steps have a progress ring; the steps ring fills up as you get closer to your daily step goal.

How does the sun and the moon work?

From sunrise to sunset the sun moves from the left end of the arc, through the top at midday, to the right end. After sunset the moon takes its place and travels back along the arc until the next sunrise. The day uses a bright daytime scene and the night a dark twilight scene.

The sun looks wrong or sunrise and sunset are not right

SunTrace works out sunrise and sunset from the last known location of the watch. If the watch has no location yet, the face uses 06:00 to 18:00 until it gets one. Keep the watch connected to your phone and, if needed, start an outdoor activity once or open Garmin's weather on the watch, then check again after a few minutes.

Does it work with the always-on display?

Yes. When the screen dims, the watch face switches to a simple black screen with only the hour marks and the hands to save battery and protect the AMOLED screen. The second hand and the sun and moon are shown only while the screen is fully on. Turn always-on on or off in the watch's own display settings.

Heart rate or Body Battery shows dashes

The values come from your watch. A dash means the watch has no reading yet: heart rate appears after the watch measures it, and Body Battery needs a recent Body Battery value from your watch.

Refunds

The payment is handled by KiezelPay (kiezelpay.com), not by Garmin, so Garmin cannot issue refunds for it. Please write to the contact below and include the code shown on your watch.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: no health data or location leaves your watch. The watch uses its last known location only to calculate sunrise and sunset. To check the trial and purchase status, the watch sends KiezelPay its unique device identifier and model number.
