SunTrace - Privacy Policy

SunTrace is an analog watch face for round Garmin watches (Connect IQ). A small sun, and after sunset a moon, moves along an arc to show the time of day.

SunTrace does not collect, store or transmit health data, location or personal information. Heart rate, Body Battery and steps are read on the watch from Garmin's own system data and are only used to draw the face. To place the sun and the moon, the watch face uses the last known location that the watch already has (from Garmin's weather data or the last activity) to work out today's sunrise and sunset. This calculation happens on the watch; the location is never saved by SunTrace and never leaves the watch. There are no accounts, analytics or advertising.

The purchase is handled by KiezelPay (kiezelpay.com). To check the trial and purchase status, the watch sends KiezelPay the watch's unique device identifier and model number. KiezelPay's own terms and privacy policy apply to that service.
